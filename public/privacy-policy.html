Privacy Policy.

How Prime Union Trucking handles personal and business information when you visit our website or request a consultation.

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Prime Union Trucking ("Prime Union Trucking," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and handles information you provide through our website, contact forms, and Facebook or Instagram lead forms. Please review it before submitting your information.

1. Information we collect

When you contact us or complete an inquiry form, we may collect:

  • Your name, telephone number, and email address.
  • Your trucking business details, such as fleet size, operating needs, business goals, and challenges.
  • Your MC or USDOT number if you choose to provide it.
  • Information you voluntarily include in a message or consultation request.
  • Limited technical information, such as browser details, IP address, and referral information, when you access our website, to the extent collected by the technologies we use.

2. How we collect information

We obtain information when you submit a form on our website, complete a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, call or email our team, or otherwise communicate with us. Certain technical information may be collected automatically by website hosting, security, or analytics tools where those tools are in use.

3. How we use information

We use the information to respond to inquiries, evaluate your requested trucking operations or fleet growth needs, arrange consultations, provide information about relevant services, maintain records of interactions, manage and improve our website and processes, and protect our services against misuse. We may also use or disclose information when necessary to comply with applicable law. We send marketing communications only as permitted by applicable law and any consent you have provided.

4. When information may be shared

We may share information with authorized team members and service providers that support our website hosting, lead management, communications, and customer service, where necessary for these functions. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect legitimate rights and security. Our practices and any third-party arrangements should be reviewed and updated if our business operations change.

5. Facebook and Instagram lead forms

If you submit information through a Meta lead form, the information you choose to provide may be shared with Prime Union Trucking so that we can respond to your request. Meta also processes information under its own terms and privacy practices. You can review Meta's Privacy Policy for more information.

6. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use essential technologies needed for operation and, where configured, analytics or advertising measurement tools. Such tools may use cookies or similar technologies. Browser settings and applicable service privacy controls may allow you to limit certain tracking. We will provide additional notices or choices where applicable law requires them.

7. Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including servicing inquiries, maintaining appropriate business records, and meeting legal requirements. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to safeguard information; however, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your privacy choices

You may contact us to ask about information we hold about you, request correction or deletion, or ask that we stop using your contact details for marketing. We will respond to requests in accordance with applicable law, subject to any lawful retention obligations. You can also opt out of marketing messages using any unsubscribe instructions provided with them.

9. Third-party websites

Our website may link to services operated by third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices, which we encourage you to review.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when our services, technology, or legal obligations change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.

11. Contact us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or requests concerning your information, contact Prime Union Trucking at 518-407-1772, or contact us through our website.
